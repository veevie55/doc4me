## Change

Store and round-trip DrawingML shape AutoFit in `TextBody`. Floating boxes measure their stories before positioning, grow along the stacking direction (height for horizontal text, width for turned text), and use the grown rectangle for text, drawing and wrapping. Measurements share the existing recursion budget and 4000 pt frame cap. Fixed-size and linked boxes retain their behavior. Inline AutoFit is preserved on save but is not resized by this change; VML-only AutoFit import is not implemented here.
